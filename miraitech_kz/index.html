About Miraitech

Our mission is to create accessible, effective, and personalized solutions for rehabilitation and sports using AI and sensor technology.

We tackle the high cost and limited availability of rehabilitation solutions with our smart insoles, offering precise biomechanical gait analysis, AI-driven diagnostics, and rehabilitation acceleration.

Technology & Achievements

Innovative Sensors: Our smart insoles integrate TENG & PENG-based wearable technology for real-time gait analysis.

Patents & Research: 2 patents granted (2022), 2 pending (2024), and 14 Q1 research publications.

Market Potential: The global market is valued at $3.4B (2024), projected to reach $5.4B by 2032.

Partnerships: Collaborations with rehabilitation centers, clinics, and sports organizations to bring our technology to real-world applications.
